PLAYABLE DEMO — WRITTEN 100% IN THE VOTYER LANGUAGE

CRYSTAL QUEST

Collect all 6 crystals, wade through the underground water channel, avoid the guards, survive the stalker, then reach the violet portal.
This whole game is the <script type="text/votyer"> block on this page — view source to see it.

WASD move  ·  mouse look  ·  space / click fire  ·  M minimap  ·  ESC pause

Items with handlers

Every crystal has an on take handler written in votyer — score, sounds and messages are all driven by the script below.

Enemy AI

Guards patrol and chase when they see you. Stalkers are faster and hit harder. You can shoot them — they have hp.

Water & torches (v1.1)

The pool is a water block with ~ tiles — animated waves, sky reflection and splash physics come free. Torches are glow on items that cast real light.

World rules

The exit portal stays locked until every item is taken — implemented with the on all items taken rule.
