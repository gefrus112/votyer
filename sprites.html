Built-in sprites

Every shape below is generated procedurally by the engine at runtime — no image files, no sprite sheets, no downloads. Give any item or enemy a shape and a color and it appears in your world, lit by the dynamic light system and casting a soft ground shadow. Everything you see here is drawn by the same code that draws your game.

How to use a shape

Attach a shape to an item or an enemy declaration. The color tints the artwork, and glow on makes the sprite emit real light into the scene — walls and floors near it pick up its color.

item "ruby" {
    char "R"
    shape crystal        // any shape from this page
    color #ff5566
    glow on              // emits light + draws attention
    value 50
    on take {
        score += 50
        sound pickup
        say "The ruby is yours!"
    }
}
15 shapes, infinite looks. Combine any shape with any #hex color and glow to get a completely different feel — a torch in orange lights a corridor, the same torch in cyan reads as alien technology. Try them live in the playground.

Decor & lighting tips

Shapes like barrel, mushroom and skull are perfect as non-interactive dressing: place them with their own map character and no on take handler, and the player simply walks past them. torch is the workhorse of the lighting system — a glowing torch item placed in a dark corridor becomes a flickering warm light source that the renderer picks up automatically, no extra configuration needed.

Enemies can glow too. A ghost with glow on announces itself around corners before you see it — an inexpensive but powerful tension builder for horror-flavored maps. Combine a low fog value with glowing enemies for the classic "red eyes in the dark" effect.
